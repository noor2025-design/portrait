# Project Overview
This project was to find two artwork pieces from other designers or artists and create a visual interpretation of the new artworks "in conversation" inspired by the references. I chose graphic designer Tyler Spangler who is an incredible designer from Southern California who does a lot of personal work but has also collaborated with major brands and fashion houses like Gucci, Coach, and Adobe. Tyler's work is heavily rooted in color and linework, especially his personal work. The constraints of this project was that I could only use HTML, CSS and some Javascript so that everything was designed using only elements, no images or external graphics.
## Concept / Process
I chose two pieces that felt like they were in conversation with each other, both rooted in linework. My visual interpretation is centered around movement and motion. I asked myself, if this artwork could move or animate, what would it be doing? I noticed that Tyler's personal work often has an 80s and 90s retro design aesthetic and I wanted to capture that feeling in my own piece. I also incorporated some of Tyler's own quotes into the design along with shapes that frequently appear throughout his work. 
## Design System
Tyler uses vibrant color systems so I chose a palette that reflects that: neon green, neon yellow, magenta pink, and black for the text. Most of the design is built around linework inspired by the two art pieces, which also ties into that retro 80s/90s aesthetic. I chose typography that feels reminiscent of retro games and early computers, which fit naturally into the design system.
I also wanted to showcase the two pieces next to each other to show how they're in conversation. Despite being inspired by two different artworks, they feel like they belong together.
## User Experience 
The first piece, Movement, is inspired by Tyler's personal work which was named "Untitled" because it had no title which features a lot of linework with blobs of what looks like colorful paint layered on top. My interpretation was to use shapes since they are easier to work with in code, arranged in a grid in vibrant colors. Users can click on the shapes and they kind of morph into each other and return back to their original state.
The second piece, Motion, is inspired by Tyler's artwork "Keep Digging" which features linework with a window that partially reveals a pattern behind it. I took that hide and show moment and made it the interaction for my own visual interpretation. I also included quotes from Tyler that are inspirational but also tell a story. It starts with "You Owe You" like you owe it to yourself to work on yourself and become the best version of you, then "Make" "Some" "Thing", and then along the way you will hit barriers and obstacles so you have to pause and ask "Wait, WTF?"
Overall this experience is meant to be fun, visually engaging, and nice to look at.
